Our Care

A calm, secure home setting for your dog — Annandale, Inner West

We're insured and have first aid for pets.

Home daycare

Day visits in our calm home environment — playtime, rest, daily walks and plenty of attention, in a small group so every dog is known and cared for. Ideal for puppies, older dogs, and everyone in between.

Senior dogs & special needs

Older dogs and dogs with health needs get a gentler routine — extra rest, calm company, wellness checks through the day, and oral medication given if needed. Just tell us what your dog needs.

Puppies

Puppies under 6 months get the extra structure they need — frequent toilet breaks, calm introductions to the group, and notes back to you on how their socialisation is coming along.

Free meet & greet

Before anything else, we invite you and your dog to come and visit. A relaxed 30 minutes — your dog meets Pansi, gets a sniff around, and you meet Andressa and ask anything you'd like. No charge, no pressure, no obligation.
