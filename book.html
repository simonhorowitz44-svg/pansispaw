Pricing notes & add-ons

Senior Care add-on: +$12/day. For dogs aged 10+ or with health needs requiring a modified routine.

Puppy Care add-on: +$12/day. For puppies under 6 months — frequent toilet breaks, gentle group intro, photo updates and behaviour notes.

Medication administration (oral): +$5/visit. Owner provides clearly labelled medication.

Pickup & dropoff: $20 each way within 5 km · $30 each way 5–10 km · beyond 10 km by arrangement.

Special diet preparation: +$3/day. If owner provides specialised food.

Public holiday surcharge: +$25/day on NSW public holidays.

Late pickup: +$10 per 30 mins after 5:30 PM (15-minute grace, capped at $40/day).

Cancellation: Cancellations within 24 hours of booking incur 50% fee. Genuine emergencies excepted.
