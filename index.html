Meet Andressa

My name is Andressa, and I am the founder of Pansi's Paw Home Daycare — a boutique home dog daycare in Annandale, lovingly serving families across Sydney's Inner West.

Dogs have been part of my world for as long as I can remember. Growing up with them taught me something that has stayed with me my whole life: dogs don't just need care — they need presence, patience, safety, and love.

Pansi's Paw was born from that belief.

What started as unconditional love for my own Labrador, Pansi, gently grew into a small home-based dog daycare created for families who want something more personal than a large facility. I wanted to build a space where dogs feel calm, understood, and truly at home — not overstimulated, not overlooked.

At Pansi's Paw, we offer small group dog daycare in Annandale, intentionally limited so every dog receives real attention, affection, and supervision. Each day is thoughtfully structured with enrichment, outdoor park walks, rest time, and plenty of quiet moments in a peaceful home environment.

Here, your dog is welcomed into my home — not into a kennel or commercial centre — but into a safe, warm space built around emotional wellbeing and gentle care.

My philosophy is simple:

  • A calm and secure home setting
  • Small groups for personalised, attentive care
  • Respect for each dog's unique personality and routine
  • Daily walks and meaningful enrichment
  • Honest, transparent communication with pet parents

I understand how hard it is to leave your dog in someone else's hands. They are family. That trust means everything to me. That is why I keep things personal, consistent, and open — with regular updates so you can feel confident and at ease.

If you are looking for a softer, more personal kind of dog care in the Inner West of Sydney, Pansi's Paw offers a gentler alternative.

Together with Pansi, I've created a space where dogs feel safe, balanced, and genuinely loved — just as they should.

Thank you for trusting this little dream of mine 🤍

— Andressa
